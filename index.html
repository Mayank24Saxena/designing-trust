    The ordinary days

    A test that works in a laboratory can still fail in a kitchen. This project asks what it takes for a person, alone at home, to trust a capsule they swallow and a phone that tells them it is working.

    Trust here is not a feeling the design asks for. It is something the design has to earn, one small certainty at a time.
