Designing Trust in Self-Administered Colorectal Cancer Screening: a note before you enter

Hello, and thank you for being here.

What’s inside is six months of my life: a small capsule, a phone, and a long question about what makes something feel safe enough to trust.

Much of it was made with people who trusted me with their stories, and with a company that let me inside a product still being born. So I keep it behind a quiet door.

If you’re holding the book, the code is on the page you scanned.

If you’re not, write to me. I would love to let you in.
